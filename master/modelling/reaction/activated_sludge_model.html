Activated Sludge Model (ASM3h)#

The Activated Sludge Model 3 extended (ASM3h) is a comprehensive reaction model for simulating biological wastewater treatment processes. It belongs to the Activated Sludge Models (ASM) family, a set of standardized models developed by the International Water Association (IWA). The ASM3h model describes the fate of 13 biochemical components in wastewater treatment systems.

Model Components#

The ASM3h model tracks 13 biochemical components representing species and compounds in wastewater:

Abbreviation

Component

\(S_O\)

Dissolved oxygen

\(S_S\)

Readily biodegradable substrate

\(S_{NH}\)

Ammonium

\(S_{NO}\)

Nitrite and nitrate

\(S_{N2}\)

Dinitrogen, released by denitrification

\(S_{ALK}\)

Alkalinity, bicarbonate

\(S_I\)

Soluble inert organic

\(X_I\)

Inert Particulate organic

\(X_S\)

Slowly biodegradable substrate

\(X_H\)

Heterotrophic biomass

\(X_{STO}\)

Organics stored by heterotrophs

\(X_A\)

Autotrophic, nitrifying biomass

\(X_{MI}\)

Mineral particulate matter from biomass

For the reaction equations, \(c_i^\kappa\) denotes the concentration of component \(i\) in the bulk (\(\kappa=b\)) or particle liquid (\(\kappa=p\)) phase. With fractionation, \(S_S\) and \(S_I\) are each split into adsorbable and non-adsorbable components, denoted by \(S_{S,\mathrm{ad}}\), \(S_{S,\mathrm{non-ad}}\), \(S_{I,\mathrm{ad}}\), and \(S_{I,\mathrm{non-ad}}\). The configuration names SS and SI refer to the non-adsorbable components when fractionation is enabled; in the rate equations, \(c_{S_S}^\kappa = c_{S_S,\mathrm{non-ad}}^\kappa\) and \(c_{S_I}^\kappa = c_{S_I,\mathrm{non-ad}}^\kappa\) in this case. Their phase-specific totals are

\[c_{S_S,\mathrm{tot}}^\kappa = c_{S_S,\mathrm{non-ad}}^\kappa + c_{S_S,\mathrm{ad}}^\kappa, \qquad c_{S_I,\mathrm{tot}}^\kappa = c_{S_I,\mathrm{non-ad}}^\kappa + c_{S_I,\mathrm{ad}}^\kappa.\]

Without fractionation, \(c_{S_S,\mathrm{tot}}^\kappa = c_{S_S}^\kappa\) and \(c_{S_I,\mathrm{tot}}^\kappa = c_{S_I}^\kappa\).

The net flux for each component is computed using the stoichiometric matrix \(S\) and a reaction rate vector \(\varphi_j(c)\), where subscript \(j\) denotes the reaction index. The ASM3h model comprises twelve biochemical reactions and an aeration term, so \(S \in \mathbb{R}^{13 \times 13}\). With fractionation, two further storage reactions act on the adsorbable substrate and two further components are tracked, giving \(S \in \mathbb{R}^{15 \times 15}\). All reactions are detailed below.

Reaction Description

Rate Equation

Anoxic storage of \(S_S\)

\(k_{STO} \cdot \eta_{h,NO3} \cdot \frac{K_{h,O2}}{c_{S_O}^\kappa + K_{h,O2}} \cdot \frac{c_{S_{NO}}^\kappa}{c_{S_{NO}}^\kappa + K_{h,NO3}} \cdot \frac{c_{S_S}^\kappa}{c_{S_S,\mathrm{tot}}^\kappa + K_{h,SS}} \cdot c_{X_H}^\kappa\)

\(r_4\)

Aerobic growth of \(X_H\)

\(\mu_H \cdot \frac{c_{S_O}^\kappa}{c_{S_O}^\kappa + K_{h,O2}} \cdot \frac{c_{X_{STO}}^\kappa/c_{X_H}^\kappa}{c_{X_{STO}}^\kappa/c_{X_H}^\kappa + K_{h,STO}} \cdot \frac{c_{S_{NH}}^\kappa}{c_{S_{NH}}^\kappa + K_{h,NH4}} \cdot \frac{c_{S_{ALK}}^\kappa}{c_{S_{ALK}}^\kappa + K_{h,ALK}} \cdot c_{X_H}^\kappa\)

\(r_5\)

Anoxic growth of \(X_H\) (denitrification)

\(\mu_H \cdot \eta_{h,NO3} \cdot \frac{K_{h,O2}}{c_{S_O}^\kappa + K_{h,O2}} \cdot \frac{c_{S_{NO}}^\kappa}{c_{S_{NO}}^\kappa + K_{h,NO3}} \cdot \frac{c_{X_{STO}}^\kappa/c_{X_H}^\kappa}{c_{X_{STO}}^\kappa/c_{X_H}^\kappa + K_{h,STO}} \cdot \frac{c_{S_{NH}}^\kappa}{c_{S_{NH}}^\kappa + K_{h,NH4}} \cdot \frac{c_{S_{ALK}}^\kappa}{c_{S_{ALK}}^\kappa + K_{h,ALK}} \cdot c_{X_H}^\kappa\)

\(r_6\)

Aerobic endogenous respiration of \(X_H\)

\(b_H \cdot \frac{c_{S_O}^\kappa}{c_{S_O}^\kappa + K_{h,O2}} \cdot c_{X_H}^\kappa\)

\(r_7\)

Anoxic endogenous respiration of \(X_H\)

\(b_H \cdot \eta_{h,end} \cdot \frac{K_{h,O2}}{c_{S_O}^\kappa + K_{h,O2}} \cdot \frac{c_{S_{NO}}^\kappa}{c_{S_{NO}}^\kappa + K_{h,NO3}} \cdot c_{X_H}^\kappa\)

\(r_8\)

Aerobic respiration of internal cell storage products

\(b_H \cdot \frac{c_{S_O}^\kappa}{c_{S_O}^\kappa + K_{h,O2}} \cdot c_{X_{STO}}^\kappa\)

\(r_9\)

Anoxic respiration of internal cell storage products

\(b_H \cdot \eta_{h,end} \cdot \frac{K_{h,O2}}{c_{S_O}^\kappa + K_{h,O2}} \cdot \frac{c_{S_{NO}}^\kappa}{c_{S_{NO}}^\kappa + K_{h,NO3}} \cdot c_{X_{STO}}^\kappa\)

\(r_{10}\)

Aerobic growth of \(X_A\)

\(\mu_{AUT} \cdot \frac{c_{S_O}^\kappa}{c_{S_O}^\kappa + K_{N,O2}} \cdot \frac{c_{S_{NH}}^\kappa}{c_{S_{NH}}^\kappa + K_{N,NH4}} \cdot \frac{c_{S_{ALK}}^\kappa}{c_{S_{ALK}}^\kappa + K_{N,ALK}} \cdot c_{X_A}^\kappa\)

\(r_{11}\)

Aerobic endogenous respiration of \(X_A\)

\(b_{AUT} \cdot \frac{c_{S_O}^\kappa}{c_{S_O}^\kappa + K_{h,O2}} \cdot c_{X_A}^\kappa\)

\(r_{12}\)

Anoxic endogenous respiration of \(X_A\)

\(b_{AUT} \cdot \eta_{N,end} \cdot \frac{K_{h,O2}}{c_{S_O}^\kappa + K_{h,O2}} \cdot \frac{c_{S_{NO}}^\kappa}{c_{S_{NO}}^\kappa + K_{h,NO3}} \cdot c_{X_A}^\kappa\)

\(r_{13}\)

Aeration

\(\frac{iO_2}{V}\)

\(r_{14}\)

Aerobic storage of \(S_{S,\mathrm{ad}}\) (optional)

\(k_{STO} \cdot \frac{c_{S_O}^\kappa}{c_{S_O}^\kappa + K_{h,O2}} \cdot \frac{c_{S_S,\mathrm{ad}}^\kappa}{c_{S_S,\mathrm{tot}}^\kappa + K_{h,SS}} \cdot c_{X_H}^\kappa\)

\(r_{15}\)

Anoxic storage of \(S_{S,\mathrm{ad}}\) (optional)

\(k_{STO} \cdot \eta_{h,NO3} \cdot \frac{K_{h,O2}}{c_{S_O}^\kappa + K_{h,O2}} \cdot \frac{c_{S_S,\mathrm{ad}}^\kappa}{c_{S_S,\mathrm{tot}}^\kappa + K_{h,SS}} \cdot \frac{c_{S_{NO}}^\kappa}{c_{S_{NO}}^\kappa + K_{h,NO3}} \cdot c_{X_H}^\kappa\)

Reactions \(r_{14}\) and \(r_{15}\) are only active when 15 components are defined (including \(S_{S,\mathrm{ad}}\) and \(S_{I,\mathrm{ad}}\)).

Note that the endogenous respiration of the autotrophs, \(r_{11}\) and \(r_{12}\), uses the oxygen saturation coefficient of the heterotrophs, \(K_{h,O2}\), and not \(K_{N,O2}\).

Temperature Dependence#

Kinetic rates are corrected for temperature using Arrhenius-type expressions:

\[k(T) = k_{20} \cdot \exp\left(-\theta \cdot (20 - T)\right)\]

with temperature coefficients:

  • \(\theta = 0.04\) for hydrolysis

  • \(\theta = 0.06952\) for heterotrophic processes (\(K_{STO}\), \(\mu_H\), \(b_H\))

  • \(\theta = 0.105\) for autotrophic processes (\(\mu_{AUT}\), \(b_{AUT}\))

Configuration in CADET#

For configuration details, see Activated Sludge Model (ASM3h).

Warning

When using ASM3h in the liquid phase of a CSTR, the bulk liquid volume must remain constant throughout the simulation. To prescribe this volume, set the CSTR parameter LIQUID_VOLUME. In every section, the inlet flow rate must equal the sum of the outlet and filter flow rates.

Aeration Strategy#

The ASM3h model includes an aeration reaction (\(r_{13}\)) that is activated when both ASM3_IO2 and ASM3_V are set. The aeration rate is computed as \(iO_2 / V\). To disable aeration, omit either parameter. For more flexible aeration handling, model oxygen input explicitly via the Inlet unit operation (see Inlet).

Substrate Fractionation#

The organic substrates can be fractionated into adsorbable and non-adsorbable components via ASM3_FSI_AD and ASM3_FSS_AD. This requires at least 15 components (including SI_ad and SS_ad, corresponding to \(S_{I,\mathrm{ad}}\) and \(S_{S,\mathrm{ad}}\)) and enables coupling with binding models for adsorptive interactions during biological treatment. If fewer than 15 components are defined, adsorbed components are disabled.

When fractionation is enabled, reactions \(r_{14}\) and \(r_{15}\) become active for storage of adsorbable substrate \(S_{S,\mathrm{ad}}\).

Implementation Notes#

  • Component indices can be customized via ASM3_COMP_IDX if components are ordered differently or offset.

  • Some kinetic terms contain ratios of particulate substrate to biomass. A smooth lower bound on the biomass concentration is used to regularize these ratios:

\[c_{X_H,\mathrm{lb}} = c_{X_H,\mathrm{min}} + \frac{1}{2}\left(c_{X_H} - c_{X_H,\mathrm{min}} + \sqrt{\left(c_{X_H} - c_{X_H,\mathrm{min}}\right)^2 + \varepsilon_X^2}\right)\]
